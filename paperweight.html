Paperweight

A calm, private budget app for tracking income, spending, and savings—month by month.

What it does

  • Monthly budgeting with categories and remaining balance
  • Spending targets—one category or many—plus recurring targets
  • Recurring income and expense entries
  • Insights: trends, lifetime breakdown, and target adherence
  • Themes with light and dark modes
  • Face ID / passcode lock
  • CSV/JSON backups and manual iCloud Save / Get transfer

Privacy

Your budget data stays on your device. No account required. Optional Cloud Transfer writes one overwriteable snapshot to your iCloud Drive app container when you tap Save to iCloud.
